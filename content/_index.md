After School Special is a Chicagoland cover band built almost entirely out of
Stevenson High School retired faculty; several members spent decades teaching
there before finding a second act on stage. What started in the early '90s as
"the faculty band," playing a variety of school events, has grown into a
full-fledged bar band with a setlist that swings from AC/DC to Adele, Led
Zeppelin to Miley Cyrus. They now play the north and west Chicagoland area and
often donate their services to organizations in need of entertainment such as
Pint for Kim, Project Relo, Naperville Salute, and SHS's Odyssey where they
opened for both Shirley King and Davey Jones of the Monkees. Now, after
weathering a rotating cast of bassists and singers, they have picked up a
dedicated sound engineer and have never sounded better. These days they're
determined not to be a typical dad band, and are having entirely too much fun
proving it.

To date, these are people we have performed with. Some were permanent members
and others sat in from time to time: We played with: Alice Brockway and then
Nuteson, Jeff "Manimal" Keepper, Rick DuFour, Sue Penrod, a choir containing
Dan Galloway and Barb Zivkovic among others, Bill MacNamara, Amanda LeBrun,
Jim Helen, Scott Williams, Tommy and Bruce Fiztgerald, Maddie Vogler, Tim
Beyer and Jeremy Beyer.

After School Special band is all about teachers. We started playing together in
the 1990's to entertain our students at school functions like "Showcase," the
student talent show, and "Odyssey," the all-school festival of the arts. We
played private parties and donated our services to organizations operating
fundraisers like Matt's Mile in Prairie Crossing and St. Gilbert's church in
Grayslake. We keep that as an operating philosophy to this day. If you have an
organization that needs a band, but you don't have a ton of cash to pay for
that band, we can step in.

Right around the time Jeremy joined the band we started looking to play out in
the real world. Our first bar gig was at Mickey Finn's in Libertyville. I
credit Brian Grano for taking a chance on us. It was the push we needed to get
into regular gigs.

We love playing out. Bring us to your venue next! We play bars, breweries,
private parties, and fundraisers throughout Lake County and the northwest
Chicago suburbs, including Libertyville, Buffalo Grove, Arlington Heights,
Mundelein, and Highland Park.
