Chip grew up around music. His mother was a piano teacher, and he learned on the same piano she did, still in the family nearly 90 years later. He played trombone through high school and taught himself bass because a friend's band needed one ("four strings, how hard can it be?"). These days he's on drums, a job he backed into when the band's longtime drummer retired, and it turns out growing up with a drummer brother pays off eventually. He also somehow knows the words to every song ever written, so don't be surprised if he's singing along even when it's not his verse.
