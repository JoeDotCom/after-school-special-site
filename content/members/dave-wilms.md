Dave got his start courtesy of his dad, who bought him a 1950s Gibson Les Paul for $100 and built his own amp and speaker cabinet from a kit. In college at Indiana University he played to a crowd of thousands at the legendary Dun Meadow and opened twice for a then-unknown John "Cougar" Mellencamp. He met Don the summer before joining Stevenson's science department and the two have been playing together for 39 years. Now retired, Dave works in solar power sales and spends his downtime sailing and traveling.
