Joe is the reason you can actually hear the band and the reason the band can (occasionally) hear itself. He runs sound, wrangles cables and records every show so everyone can relive their finest guitar riffs in high definition. He'll pick up a tambourine or shaker when nobody's looking, but don't cheer too loud or he'll get spooked and scurry away backstage.
