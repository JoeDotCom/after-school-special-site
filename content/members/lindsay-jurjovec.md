Lindsay joined After School Special right before the world shut down. Her first show was February 2020 at the Humble Pub in Highwood, days before COVID sent everyone home. She stepped in when longtime singer Alice Knutson joined the choir of Milwaukee Symphony. It took about one song, "Me and Bobby McGee," for the band to know she was the right fit. Lindsay brings a raw, instinctive energy to the band's vocals, and isn't afraid to belt out a classic rock anthem exactly as written, pronouns and all.
