Mike's musical career has taken a few detours: a childhood spent switching between a French horn he didn't ask for and a tuba he did, a few decades off entirely, and a comeback that started at 61 when his wife suggested he trade his eBay tuba for a bass guitar. He joined After School Special when Don and Dave went to an open jam at a local bar, saw Mike play and offered him an audition. Off stage, he's also been known to hit 176 mph on a motorcycle, which tracks for a guy who insists he's "not very interesting."
